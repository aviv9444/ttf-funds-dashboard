Indxx Super Institutional Investors Holdings 13F Index

Overview

The Indxx Super Institutional Investors Holdings 13F Index measures the performance of the most liquid companies that are being held by asset management entities, investment banks, pension funds, hedge funds,insurance investment arms and others who are legally obligated to report their holdings through 13F filings as per 13F filings regulations. The index has been backtested to March 31, 2021 and has a live calculation date of July 30, 2025.
